ASSE 6015 — Bulk Medical Gas Systems Installer

Home / Certifications / ASSE 6015

The ASSE 6015 Bulk Medical Gas Systems Installer Certification is for individuals who install bulk or micro-bulk systems—also called Cryogenic Fluid Central Supply Systems—at health care facilities. The certification applies to installers, brazers, and welders who work on these systems or their components.

Notice: CGA will no longer provide certification services for ASSE 6015/CGA M-1. Please contact MGTI for more information.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Documented experience in the installation of bulk medical gas systems, in accordance with their firm's standard operating procedures (SOPs)
  • Completion of a minimum 32-hour training course, conducted by an ASSE 6055 certified instructor
  • Passing scores on both the written and practical examinations
