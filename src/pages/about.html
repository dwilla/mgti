About the Medical Gas Training Institute

Home / About

The Medical Gas Training Institute functions as an independent certifying organization for the medical gas sector, delivering testing and credentials for professionals across the industry. The organization prioritizes quality assurance and practical application in its examination frameworks.

Operating under ISO Standard 17024 guidelines for personnel certification, the institute maintains rigorous standards for organizational integrity and exam development procedures. Our mission is to provide comprehensive certification services efficiently and effectively for the medical gas industry.

Program Development

The certification programs were developed by prominent medical gas and vacuum systems specialists. The Certification Scheme Committee is made up of ASSE 6050 and 6055 certified medical gas instructors, industry experts, and highly qualified educators. Programs address professionals involved in design, installation, inspection, testing, operation, service, and repair functions.

Organizational Structure

The organizational framework includes examination committees for each certification and an oversight committee. MGTI's certification exams are developed, updated, and maintained by the examination committees. Staff includes two in-house exam developers holding advanced educational degrees.

Additional details about our policies, procedures, and governance appear in the Policies and Procedures documentation.
