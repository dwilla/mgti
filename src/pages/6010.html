ASSE 6010 — Medical Gas Systems Installer

Home / Certifications / ASSE 6010

The ASSE 6010 Medical Gas Systems Installer Certification is available for people with experience in the plumbing/piping fields who are qualified to install medical gas piping, equipment, and components at health care facilities.

Includes ASME IX Brazing Certification: The ASSE 6010 certification includes the ASME IX Brazing Certification, requiring candidates to pass both a written and practical brazing examination.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Minimum four years of documented practical experience in plumbing or mechanical piping system installation
  • Completion of a 32-hour training course covering ASSE Standard 6010 and ASME IX Brazing, delivered by an instructor holding current ASSE 6050 certification
  • Successful passage of the written examination
  • Successful passage of the practical (ASME IX Brazing) examination

Experience prerequisites are verified by your attestation on the Program Application, and attestations are subject to audit by MGTI.

The Written Examination

  • 100 questions, multiple-choice and true/false
  • Four hours to complete
  • Open book — you may use NFPA 99 during the examination
  • 70% or greater required to pass

The examination is written to NFPA 99: Health Care Facilities Code, 2024 edition. MGTI always examines against the most recent edition of the code. The certification awarded covers the 2012 through 2024 editions. Content is also drawn from NFPA 55, the ASSE Series 6000 Standard, and other generally accepted industry documents.

The Practical (ASME IX Brazing) Examination

You braze test coupons in the flat (horizontal) and vertical upflow positions using a controlled kit supplied by MGTI. An oil-free dry nitrogen purge flows continuously throughout, and before any heat is applied you verify with an oxygen analyzer that oxygen within the assembly is below one percent. No flux is used. Adding filler metal to a completed vertical upflow joint counts as downflow brazing and fails the position.

Two attempts at each position. The kit supplies material for four coupons — two horizontal and two vertical — and you submit both coupons for each position. Where one coupon in a position is rejected and the other meets the acceptance criteria, the position is qualified.

Acceptance criteria: a minimum of 80 percent fill, with no visible gap between tube and coupling and no void at the cap. The grounds on which a coupon is rejected are listed in the instructions supplied with your kit — read them before you braze, not after.

MGTI does not assess its own candidates' coupons. Completed coupons are packed, sealed, and shipped to an independent third-party laboratory, where they are sectioned and visually examined by a Certified Welding Inspector against published criteria. Candidate paperwork travels separately from the coupons.

The examination is witnessed throughout by an Authorized Representative or proctor, who confirms your identity from valid government-issued identification and signs the Brazer Qualification Record. No practice brazing takes place during the examination session.

Validity and Continuity

The ASSE 6010 certification is valid for three years and is renewed through MGTI's recertification program.

The ASME IX brazing qualification does not expire on a cycle. It remains in effect for as long as you continue to use the qualified procedure and document that use — a brazed joint at intervals not exceeding six months. It lapses only when continuity is broken. Because the two run on different clocks, you may hold a current brazing qualification alongside a lapsed certification, or the reverse.
