MGTI certifications are valid for three years. The recertification programs enable certified professionals to maintain their credential status based on current code editions including NFPA 99, NFPA 55, and CGA M-1.

Why three years. ASSE Series 6000 requires recertification against subsequent editions of NFPA 99, and NFPA 99 is issued on a three-year cycle. A three-year certification period therefore tracks the code cycle, so that a certified person is never more than one edition of the governing code behind current practice.

Recertification Requirements

  • Hold active certification and complete the recertification program before your certification expires
  • Submit your renewal application 30 days before the expiration date shown on your wall certificate and certification card
  • Complete a four-hour recertification training course addressing code changes since your last certification, delivered by an instructor holding current ASSE 6050 certification (ASSE 6055 for the bulk cryogenic certifications)
  • Pass the recertification examination with the required minimum score for your certification level

The recertification course and examination are based on subsequent editions of industry codes and standards, changes in technology, and other changes in industry practice since your last certification. Recertification examinations may be written or electronic, are open book, and are not proctored.

MGTI may send email notices of pending expiration. However, you are solely responsible for keeping your certification current. Keep your contact information up to date; MGTI is not responsible for communications not received due to incorrect contact information in your record. Current renewal applications and pricing are at medgascerts.com.

Minimum Passing Scores

70% required:
  • ASSE 6005 — Generalist
  • ASSE 6010 — Installer
  • ASSE 6015 — Bulk Installer
  • ASSE 6020 — Inspector
  • ASSE 6040 — Maintenance
85% required:
  • ASSE 6030 — Verifier
  • ASSE 6035 — Bulk Verifier
  • ASSE 6050 — Instructor

Minimum passing scores are set by the ASSE Series 6000 Standard and are stated in the candidate handbook issued for your certification. Confirm the score for your certification in your handbook before you sit the examination.

Brazing Continuity — ASSE 6010 and 6015

Your ASME IX brazing qualification does not expire on a cycle. It remains in effect for as long as you continue to use the qualified procedure and that use is documented. It lapses only when continuity is broken.

You must document that you have performed a brazed joint using the qualified procedure at intervals not exceeding six (6) months. Keeping that log is your responsibility. At renewal you provide the dates and witnesses for brazing performed over the preceding three years.

If you have not kept your continuity documentation up to date, you will need to retest when you renew. Because the brazing qualification and the ASSE certification run on different clocks, you may hold a current brazing qualification alongside a lapsed certification, or the reverse.

Certification Transfer

MGTI accepts valid ASSE 6000 certifications from other third-party certification agencies. Approved transfers require completion of MGTI's recertification program. Applications must include:

  • Current certification documentation from the originating agency
  • Brazing continuity proof (for installers and brazers)

A person transferring a current ASSE 6010 certification may bring with it the brazing qualification held under the issuing body's procedure. MGTI recognizes that qualification; it does not adopt, reissue, or re-badge it — it remains a qualification made under the issuing body's procedure and record, and it must continue to be maintained under the six-month continuity rule above. A transferred qualification whose continuity log has lapsed is not current, and MGTI does not treat it as current.

A new ASSE 6010 certification is different. MGTI does not accept a brazing qualification obtained elsewhere in place of its own for the purpose of issuing a new MGTI ASSE 6010 certification. A candidate seeking ASSE 6010 from MGTI performs the brazing qualification under MGTI's own Brazing Procedure Specification.
