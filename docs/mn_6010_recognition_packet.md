## Cover Statement

The Medical Gas Training Institute (MGTI) is a personnel certification
body for the medical gas and vacuum systems trade, administering the
ASSE Series 6000 certification programs (including ASSE 6010 Medical Gas
Systems Installer) and the ASME Section IX brazing qualification. MGTI's
certification programs, governance structure, and quality controls are
designed and operated in alignment with the framework of ISO/IEC 17024 —
*General Requirements for Bodies Operating Certification of Persons* —
covering impartiality, confidentiality, examination development and
validation, and ongoing program review.

## 1. Accreditation Certificate and Accrediting Body

MGTI is not currently accredited by ANSI, IAS, or another third-party
accreditation body. In lieu of a formal accreditation certificate, MGTI
offers the following as evidence of a functioning, ISO 17024-aligned
quality system:

- A Certification Scheme Committee, appointed by the MGTI Board of
  Directors, responsible for developing and regularly reviewing all
  components of the certification process (P&P §6.1)
- A biannual audit — conducted by the Certification Scheme Committee —
  assessing both MGTI's adherence to its own Policies and Procedures and
  the continued alignment of those Policies and Procedures with ISO
  Standard 17024 (P&P §6.1)
- Documented impartiality, confidentiality, and conflict-of-interest
  controls applicable to all personnel involved in testing and
  certification (P&P §7, §8)
- A defined examination development, validation, and annual review
  process involving industry experts and, where engaged, psychometric
  review (P&P §3.2, §3.4)

## 3. ASSE 6010 Certification Scheme Description

### 3.1 Prerequisites and Eligibility

Candidates must complete a Program Application, verifying eligibility
and prerequisite practical experience, prior to entering the
certification program. Experience prerequisites are based on the ASSE
International standard and are designed to ensure candidates have the
field experience necessary to install medical gas systems without
compromising client/patient safety. MGTI also requires completion of a
32-hour training course, led by a certified instructor, prior to taking
the certification exam (P&P §2.2, §2.3).

### 3.2 Examination Development and Validation

Examination content is based on the ASSE Series 6000 Professional
Qualifications Standard, NFPA 99 (*Health Care Facilities Code*), and
other generally accepted industry documents and practices, with input
from industry professionals (P&P §3.1). Exams are developed and
maintained by MGTI's program administrator in coordination with exam
committees of industry experts, with validation via review by
educational and/or psychometric experts. Item/question analysis is
performed on an ongoing basis using candidate challenge feedback and
statistical analysis of exam scores, and examinations are formally
reviewed at least annually — or whenever codes are updated or exam items
are found to be imperfect (P&P §3.2, §3.4).

### 3.3 Practical Skills Assessment — Brazing Qualification

The ASSE 6010 program includes a practical brazing examination. Practical
exams are conducted under the conditions described in MGTI's Medical Gas
Brazing Qualifications instruction manual, and all brazing coupons are
sent to and inspected by a certified welding inspector (P&P §3.5).
Candidates must achieve 80% or greater penetration on both the vertical
and horizontal brazing positions, as qualified by an independent testing
laboratory and certified welding inspector (P&P §3.8). See Section 7
below for the conformance basis of this process against NFPA 99.

### 3.4 Recertification and Continuing Competency

Certifications are generally valid for three (3) years. Renewal requires
a Certification Renewal Application, a four (4)-hour training course, and
a passing score on a re-certification exam, which is updated to reflect
current codes, standards, and industry practices. Candidates who fail a
retake may retake it again as soon as they are able; MGTI may require an
additional training course before a third attempt (P&P §4.2).

*Open item: candidates who fail the initial 6010 examination may now

### 3.5 Alignment with ASSE Standard 6010

MGTI's 6010 program was developed by ASSE 6050/6055-certified
instructors and is maintained by MGTI's program administrator in
coordination with the examination committee, using the ASSE 6010
Professional Qualifications Standard as its basis, together with the
current edition of NFPA 99 (P&P §1, §2, §3.1). The 32-hour training
course and written/practical examinations are structured around the
knowledge and skill areas defined for medical gas installers under ASSE
6010: system general knowledge, applications, component overview,
operating principles, pipeline installation, brazing procedures and
testing, safety precautions, troubleshooting, and routine maintenance
and repairs (P&P §2).

## 4. Impartiality, Confidentiality, and Conflict of Interest

MGTI maintains high standards of fairness and impartiality, ensuring no
applicant is given advantage over another — including with respect to
teaching, training material development, coaching, or affiliations with
organizations that have an interest in test results. All external
trainers, examiners, proctors, candidates, and board members are
required to read and sign a confidentiality and conflict-of-interest
statement; MGTI employees are bound by the same standards as a condition
of employment (P&P §7). The full text of the confidentiality/COI
agreement is provided in P&P §8, and covers confidentiality of exam
materials, restrictions on third-party disclosure, secure handling and
return of materials, and prohibition on exploiting testing involvement
for personal benefit.

## 5. Complaints, Appeals, and Certification Status Verification

**Question challenges:** Candidates may challenge exam questions via a
question challenge form; MGTI reviews and corrects confirmed errors, and
adjusts any candidate's score affected by a confirmed flawed question
(P&P §3.10).

**Appeals:** Candidates may appeal a final examination score in writing
within thirty (30) days of receiving the score. Appeals are first
decided by MGTI staff, with escalation available to the MGTI Examination
Committee Chair and finally the Executive Director (P&P §3.11).

**Infraction reporting:** MGTI accepts written reports of Professional
Standards of Conduct infractions from candidates, certificate holders,
employers, and the public, and investigates factually determinable
matters while protecting reporter confidentiality (P&P §5.3).

**Certification status verification:** MGTI provides a public
certification-status lookup so third parties can confirm whether a given
credential is current or expired.

## 6. Organizational Structure

MGTI is governed by a Board of Directors, with two subcommittees: the
Certification Scheme Committee and the Examination Committees. The
Executive Director manages day-to-day operations and is responsible for
the control and security of all certification documents, including
examinations. The Board conducts an annual review of the Executive
Director and a general internal audit of the organization. All committee
members and management are required to be familiar with ISO 17024 (P&P
§6).

The Certification Scheme Committee is responsible for developing,
reviewing, and updating certification process, rules, appeals,
revocations, and issuance/suspension procedures (excluding exam content
itself), meeting at least twice annually, with a simple majority vote
sufficient for decisions (P&P §6.1). The Examination Committees are
recruited by certification area and consulted for exam content
development and revision as needed (P&P §6.2).

## 7. Brazing Qualification Conformance with NFPA 99

MGTI's brazing practical examination is administered under the
conditions specified in MGTI's Medical Gas Brazing Qualifications
instruction manual and is based on American Society of Mechanical
Engineers (ASME) Section IX standards for brazing procedure and
performance qualification (P&P §3.1). NFPA 99 (*Health Care Facilities
Code*) requires that personnel who braze medical gas piping be qualified
under a recognized brazing procedure qualification standard; MGTI's use
of ASME Section IX as the qualification basis for its practical exam is
the mechanism by which the 6010 program satisfies that NFPA 99
requirement. All brazing coupons are inspected and qualified by a
certified welding inspector and an independent testing laboratory, with
a minimum 80% penetration requirement on both vertical and horizontal
brazing positions (P&P §3.5, §3.8).
