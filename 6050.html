ASSE 6050 — Medical Gas Systems Instructor

Home / Certifications / ASSE 6050

The ASSE 6050 Medical Gas Systems Instructor certification targets individuals who intend to teach the required classes for ASSE 6000 certifications. Candidates must demonstrate extensive experience in piping and medical gas fields, as well as practical instructional experience. The program covers installation, maintenance, testing, and code requirements per NFPA 99, NFPA 55, and related standards.

Please note: The ASSE 6050 certification does not qualify instructors for bulk cryogenic fluid systems (ASSE 6015/6035). Teaching those courses requires the separate ASSE 6055 Instructor Certification.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Minimum 10 years of documented piping installation experience, including at least 2 years in medical gas/vacuum piping systems
  • Minimum 2 years of documented practical teaching or training experience
  • Current ASSE 6010 Medical Gas Systems Installer certification
  • Completion of a 40-hour training course covering ASSE Standard 6050 and ASME IX Brazing Qualifications
  • Passing the final examination (100 questions)
