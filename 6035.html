ASSE 6035 — Bulk Medical Gas Systems Verifier

Home / Certifications / ASSE 6035

The ASSE 6035 certification is for professionals who need to conduct the testing, inspection, and verification of bulk medical gas systems at healthcare facilities. Verifiers assess the purity and integrity of bulk systems, with training covering NFPA 99, NFPA 55, CGA M-1, and related standards.

Notice: CGA will no longer provide certification services for ASSE 6035. Please contact MGTI for more information.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Minimum two years of documented practical experience in bulk medical gas system verification and/or inspection
  • Completion of a 32-hour training course addressing bulk systems and all relevant standards (reduced to 16 hours for current holders of ASSE 6030 Medical Gas Systems Verifier certification)
  • Successful passage of written and practical examinations
ASSE 6030 Holders: If you currently hold the ASSE 6030 Medical Gas Systems Verifier certification, the required training is reduced from 32 hours to 16 hours.
