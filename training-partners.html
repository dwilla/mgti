Training Partners

Home / Training Partners

MGTI works with approved training partners to deliver the required courses for ASSE 6000 certifications. Whether you prefer in-class instruction or self-paced online learning, we have options to fit your schedule.

In-Class Training Partner

Acute Medical Gas Services (AMG)

New England

Acute Medical Gas Services (AMG) is a New England-based organization offering ASSE 6000 training classes at their location and traveling to other sites. Contact AMG directly for class schedules and proposals.

Online Training Partner

Medical Gas Certifications

Online, self-paced

Medical Gas Certifications provides 24/7 self-paced web-based training for ASSE 6005, 6020, and 6040 certifications, plus recertification code updates. Ideal for candidates who need flexibility in their study schedule.

Become a Training Partner

MGTI invites ASSE 6050-certified instructors to partner as third-party certification training providers. If you are an ASSE 6050 certified instructor interested in partnering with MGTI, please reach out to us.
